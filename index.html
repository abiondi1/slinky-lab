Answer key

# of loopsCycles in 10 sLength of one loop (m)Frequency (Hz)Wavelength (m)Frequency × wavelength (m/s)

Pattern: frequency goes up by the same step each time (1×, 2×, 3×, 4× the first), wavelength shrinks (2L, L, 2L/3, L/2), and their product stays the same. That constant is the wave speed in this slinky. Student counts within about ±1 cycle are normal, since they count whole cycles.
